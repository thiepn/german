M22 instead trains **productive evidential attribution**: embed an original quotation verbatim within one's own German syntax, report an independently paraphrased claim with its original voice and evidential force intact, and explicitly exclude stronger claims that the source cannot warrant.

Four original **fictional** source dossiers, each three long paragraphs with one independently voiced, short directly quoted statement per paragraph: municipal district heating, university advising, museum provenance, and technical ventilation. Twelve literally anchored quote/interpretation decisions require an independent indirect paraphrase, a genuinely embedded verbatim original quote, and a specific restriction. After all three, learners write a coherent independent source-aware German analysis and then revise with a word-level diff. An unchanged draft or quotation edited by even a single character is not accepted. The automated checks enforce presence, scope, and independent-length—not quality, linguistic mastery or publication rights.
