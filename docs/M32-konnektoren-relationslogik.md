# M32 — G04 Konnektoren und Relationslogik

## Coverage audit G01–G13 / M01–M31
G04 M15/M21 train information structure and sentence compression; M30 trains focus-particle and negation scope; M31 trains syntactic attachment in G03. M32 instead targets **interclausal discourse relation**: concession versus cause, cause versus co-occurrence, conditional possibility versus obligation, temporal ordering versus causation, adversative contrast, additional reason, concessive hypothesis, means, and explicit limits on transferability. This is separate from G11's evidence-backed argument chains, which focus on claims and decision warrants rather than the semantic force and direction of German connectors.

## Learning and persistence
For each anchored relation, the learner chooses an ungraded reading and independently writes a faithful relation paraphrase, a tempting but unsupported opposite reading, a distinct contextual evidence statement and an explicit limit or disallowed inference. After completing all three, an independently authored multi-paragraph document and an audience-sensitive substantially revised version are required. The learner sees original source, append-only history, editorial example in details, and per-revision diff. Today, Curriculum, Progress, Archive and source return use existing navigation. Production CAS, eight IndexedDB stores and backup v3 are reused without schema changes, grading claims or migrations.
