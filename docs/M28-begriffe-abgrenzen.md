# M28 — G06 Begriffe präzise abgrenzen

## Curriculum gap
M18 explores contextual lexical ambiguity of individual lemmas. M28 instead asks learners to maintain **operational terminological boundaries throughout a complex document**, explicitly detecting and repairing scope drift and the false equivalence of superficially related concepts. It is distinct from M20 cross-source comparison, M25 numeric-statistical precision, M26 selective disclosure, M27 chronology and M11 general text editing.

Four newly authored original fictional extended dossiers (12 complete paragraphs, 12 literal quotation anchors) cover:
- Transparency, traceability and explanation in a municipal AI dispatch experiment.
- Conservation, restoration and speculative reconstruction in a museum workshop.
- Pseudonymization, anonymization and aggregation in a fictional study.
- Reproducibility, replication and transferability in a fictitious research project.

Scope labels SG-G06B-001–004 v1, C1/C2. Case-specific definitions, not claims about universally binding standards. Each anchored unit asks for a limited correct reading, an original definition, an original explicit contrast with a named adjacent term and a concrete counterexample or application. After all three anchored units, learners compose a substantial independent multi-paragraph terminology memo and provide a substantive revised version with change rationale and visible diff. Free writing remains UNASSESSED, with no CEFR credit or unreviewed automated grade.
