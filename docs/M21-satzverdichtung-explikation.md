# M21 — G04 Satzverdichtung und Explikation

## Distinct skill
Three distinct transformations of dense C1–C2 academic or administrative German are practiced in each of four original fictional documents: interpret nominalizations and embedded conditions/participial attributes; independently **expand** the syntax into accessible clauses while preserving agency, negation, modal force and time; independently **recompress** the content into an idiomatic precise sentence without copying the source. M15 trained information structure/topic focus; M12 trained modality and indirect speech; M20 trained source comparison. M21 uniquely demands **bidirectional syntax production**, not another reading multiple choice.

The four documents concern a conditional library network change, a forecast-dependent river safety response, staged approval of research funding, and a sensor-driven traffic light pilot. Each has three full-length original paragraphs and three literally anchored transformations (12 total), mixing nominalization, participial attribution, conditional temporal scopes, passive obligations, and non-entailments. All case IDs are `SG-G04C-001` through `SG-G04C-004`, version 1 and distinct from `SG-G04-*` IDs.

## Learner route and data invariants
Each operation stores an interpretation choice, original expanded sentence, original compact sentence, and explicit account of what meaning must not be lost. The first exercise is immutable in `ProductionEntry.original`; later operations are append-only `ProductionRevision` with stable paragraph anchors. After all three, the learner writes **two independent coherent text versions** for a technical/institutional audience and a public audience, supplies an account of conserved conditions and agency, and may revise both versions with a word diff.

No grade/CEFR claims, duplicate scheduler, migration, additional IndexedDB store or storage schema. Uses existing CAS `saveProductionEntry`, eight-store IndexedDB, backup v3 checksum and versioned Archive with native Today/Curriculum/Progress integration. The practice checks independent length/non-identity only; language quality and semantic adequacy remain human editorial matters.

All 4 sources and 12 decisions are `DEMO_ONLY`, `AI_DRAFT`, `ORIGINAL_DRAFT`, pending 16 independent editorial decisions. The technical code may be qualified but the authored German is not publication-approved.
